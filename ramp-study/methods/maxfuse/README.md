# MaxFuse source and publication status

The local source-complete candidate retains the study's MaxFuse wrappers,
provider preparation and frozen adapter code, including identical copies in
other components. See SOURCE_COVERAGE.json for their original paths and hashes.

This inclusion is for local completeness, not redistribution approval. The
existing LICENSE_NOTICE.md remains authoritative as a notice of unresolved
terms; the repository-wide RELEASE_GATES.md still blocks upload. Installed
upstream environments and source archives are not bundled. Obtain and use
upstream dependencies under their applicable terms.

Provider/input equivalence was checked in the historical work. The 17 MaxFuse
representations used by integration evaluation were historical outputs rather
than newly fitted outputs; source inclusion does not change that scope.

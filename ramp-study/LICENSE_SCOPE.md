# License scope for the RAMP study

At the RAMP author's explicit instruction, the original RAMP study source
code, tests, configurations and documentation are offered under **Creative
Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International
(CC-BY-NC-ND-4.0)**, matching the associated paper and the algorithm package.
The full terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

This declaration covers only rights held by the RAMP authors. It applies to
their original work throughout this directory, including `research/`,
`benchmarks/`, `methods/`, `applications/`, figure scripts and original
historical versions in `source_archive/`. Paper authors and citation details
are recorded in [AUTHORS.md](../ramp-proteomics/AUTHORS.md); the paper's author
list does not by itself establish ownership of every component.

## Third-party material and external resources

Third-party code, license texts, notices and other separately attributed
material retain their own copyright and license terms. This declaration does
not relicense them, remove existing notices, grant additional patent rights,
or grant rights to external provider data. In particular, the dependency
licenses listed in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) continue to
apply to those dependencies. The original upstream notices are preserved.

The separate MaxFuse license contains restrictions beyond redistribution of
the library itself. The authorship statement in the local driver is not a
legal determination that those restrictions cannot apply to the driver.
This CC declaration grants no Stanford/Penn rights and does not resolve the
specific MaxFuse scope question recorded in the third-party notice.

Aggregate reference tables are retained for documenting published results;
this source-code declaration does not assign a new license to provider data
or independently licensed material underlying those results.

## Historical records

The `publication` labels inside `SOURCE_COVERAGE.json` are the original
source-delivery review labels. Their generic "author terms pending" state
is superseded, for original RAMP-author material only, by this explicit
license declaration. Source paths, hashes and scientific configurations are
unchanged. Historical reports remain evidence of the state they reviewed.
